Open House Chicago · Sat., Oct. 17, 2026

Saturday South Side ride

From Pressbox to the Kam Liu Building, the Forum, and the 1 p.m. tour at Narrow Bridge Arts Club, then Oak Woods, with optional stops at Rockefeller Chapel and the Arts Bank, and back on the Lakefront Trail.

Ride 20.0 miPedaling ≈2 h 30 minStress cap LTS 2

    Graves at Oak Woods

    Locations come from OpenStreetMap volunteers, so look around once you're close. Orange marks people tied to planning and building the city. The cemetery is large, so walk your bikes on its roads.

      Notes

      • Narrow Bridge's 1 p.m. tour covers the building's carbon-neutral design. Painting to live jazz runs 2–4 p.m. if you want to linger. Enter through the black gate and garden on the north side.
      • Oak Woods is the only stop open Saturday and closed Sunday. Its office closes at 3 p.m. Saturday. A 1–2 p.m. performance there overlaps your tour.
      • The Forum has live music all day. Rockefeller Chapel has guided and self-guided visits.
